Costum Stuff ships all orders via standard shipping. This page explains when orders leave our facility, how tracking information is shared, and where to monitor your delivery progress.

## How shipping works

Orders are processed and packed within one to two business days. Once dispatched, you receive an email with your tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page.

## Tracking your order

You have two ways to follow your shipment:

- **Email notification**: Check your inbox for the dispatch email containing the tracking number and carrier link.
- **Order History**: Log in to your Costum Stuff account, go to **Account** > **Order History**, select the order, and click **Track Shipment** to view the latest status.

<Info>
  Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear after you receive the dispatch email.
</Info>

## Delivery timeline

Standard shipping delivery times vary by region. Most domestic orders arrive within five to seven business days after dispatch. International orders may take longer depending on customs and local carriers.

## Result

You can monitor your package from dispatch to delivery using the tracking number in your email or order history.
