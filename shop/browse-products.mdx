Costum Stuff offers a wide catalog of custom and personalized items. This page shows you how to navigate categories, search for specific products, and apply filters so you can quickly find what you want.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Shop">
    From the top navigation, click **Shop** to view the full product catalog. Categories like Apparel, Accessories, and Prints appear in the sidebar or top menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or select a category">
    Enter a keyword in the search bar to find items by name, theme, or style. Alternatively, click a category to browse everything inside it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Use the filter panel to narrow results by size, color, material, or price range. Filters update the grid instantly so you only see matching items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save favorites">
    Click the heart icon on any product card to add it to your favorites list. You can view saved items later from your profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Save favorites while you compare options. You can return to your list anytime from **Account** > **Profile**.
</Tip>

## Result

The product grid shows only items that match your selected filters and search terms. Saved favorites are stored in your profile for quick access later.
