If a non-custom item from Costum Stuff does not meet your expectations, you can start a return within 14 days of delivery. This page explains what is eligible, what is not, and how to submit a return request.

<Warning>
  Custom and personalized items are final sale unless they arrive defective or damaged. Because they are made to your specifications, we cannot accept returns for change of mind.
</Warning>

## Eligibility

- **Eligible**: Non-custom apparel, accessories, and prints in original condition with tags attached.
- **Not eligible**: Custom orders, worn or washed items, and items returned after the 14-day window.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Order History">
    Log in to your Costum Stuff account and navigate to **Account** > **Order History**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the order">
    Find the order containing the item you want to return and click **View Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the return">
    Click **Request Return** next to the eligible item. Choose a reason, confirm the item condition, and submit the request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship the item back">
    Once approved, print the prepaid return label from your email or order details. Pack the item securely, attach the label, and drop it off with the carrier.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

After we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund is processed to the original payment method. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund is issued.
