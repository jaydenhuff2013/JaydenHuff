Costum Stuff lets you request one-of-a-kind personalized items that are not listed in the standard catalog. This page walks you through submitting a custom order so our team can review your idea and provide a quote.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the custom order form">
    Go to **Shop** > **Custom Orders** and click **Start a Custom Order**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your idea">
    Enter a detailed description of what you want. Include the purpose, theme, text, and any special instructions that will help us understand your vision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload reference images">
    Attach sketches, photos, or inspiration images using the file uploader. Clear references help us match your expectations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose size and material">
    Select the size, material, or finish options that apply to your item. If you are unsure, note your preference and we will recommend the best fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the request">
    Review your details and click **Submit**. Our team will follow up with questions, a mockup, or a price quote before production begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Custom items are final sale unless they arrive defective or damaged. Because each piece is made to your specifications, we cannot accept returns for change of mind.
</Note>

## Result

Your custom order request is sent to our team. You will receive an email confirmation and a follow-up with next steps, usually within one to two business days.
