When you are ready to buy, Costum Stuff checkout gathers your shipping details and payment information securely. This page guides you from cart review to order confirmation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your cart">
    Click the cart icon and verify the items, quantities, and options. Remove or adjust anything before you continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your shipping address">
    Fill in the shipping form with your full name, street address, city, postal code, and country. Double-check for accuracy to avoid delivery delays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a payment method">
    Select **Credit/Debit Card** or **PayPal**. Enter your card details or log in to PayPal to authorize payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a discount code (optional)">
    If you have a discount code, enter it in the promo field and click **Apply** before placing the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your order">
    Review the final total, then click **Place Order**. You will see an order confirmation page and receive a confirmation email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Always apply discount codes before clicking **Place Order**. Promo fields cannot be adjusted after the order is submitted.
</Tip>

## Result

Your order is placed and a confirmation email is sent. You can view the order details and status anytime in your **Order History**.
