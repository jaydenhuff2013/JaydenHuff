## Install steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the file">
    Choose the installer that matches your device and save it to your computer or mobile device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the installer">
    Double-click the downloaded file on desktop, or tap the app file on mobile to begin installation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or create an account">
    Open the app, sign in to your existing account, or create a new one to get started.
  </Step>
</Steps>
