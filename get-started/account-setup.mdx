A complete Costum Stuff account lets you place orders, save payment methods, track shipments, and link your Discord profile for community perks. This page walks you through creating the account and filling in the basics.

## Prerequisites

- A valid email address you can access immediately
- A password with at least eight characters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to the Costum Stuff sign-up page. Enter your email address, choose a password, and click **Create Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Open your inbox and look for a verification message from Costum Stuff. Click the confirmation link inside. If you do not see it within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your profile">
    Open **Account Settings** and go to the **Profile** tab. Add your display name and shipping address. Saving an address now speeds up checkout later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set notification preferences">
    In the same settings area, choose how you want to hear from Costum Stuff: email for order updates, shipping alerts, and community announcements. You can change these at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a payment method (optional)">
    Go to **Payment Methods** and save a card or connect PayPal. This is optional at signup, but it makes checkout faster.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

Your account is active and verified. You can now browse products, place orders, and link your Discord account to receive community roles.
