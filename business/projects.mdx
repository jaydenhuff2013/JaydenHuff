Projects are the primary way work gets organized in Google Apps. Each project brings together tasks, team members, due dates, and milestones into a single, trackable workspace. Whether you're managing a short sprint or a long-running initiative, Google Apps gives you the flexibility to organize and monitor your work the way that suits your team best.

## Creating a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Project">
    From the left sidebar, click **Projects**, then press the **+ New Project** button in the top-right corner. You can also use the **Quick Action** shortcut on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill In Project Details">
    In the project creation dialog, enter the following:
    - **Name** — A clear, descriptive title for the project.
    - **Description** — An optional summary of the project's goals and scope.
    - **Due Date** — The target completion date. You can adjust this later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Team Members">
    Start typing a team member's name or email in the **Members** field to add them to the project. You can add multiple members. Their role within Google Apps determines what they can do inside the project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Initial Status">
    Choose a starting status for the project: **Active**, **On Hold**, or leave it as a **Draft** until you're ready to kick things off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the Project">
    Click **Create Project**. You'll be taken directly to the new project's page where you can begin adding tasks and milestones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Project Views

Google Apps offers three views for every project, allowing you to work in whichever format best suits your team's style:

**List View**
The default view. Displays all tasks in a structured, sortable list with columns for assignee, due date, priority, and status. Best for teams who prefer a linear, spreadsheet-style overview.

**Board (Kanban) View**
Displays tasks as cards organized into columns by status (e.g., To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done). Cards can be dragged between columns to update their status instantly. Ideal for visualizing workflow and spotting bottlenecks.

**Calendar View**
Shows tasks plotted on a calendar grid by due date. Useful for understanding workload distribution over time and identifying scheduling conflicts.

Switch between views using the view toggle buttons in the top-right corner of any project page. Your preferred view is saved per project.

<Tip>
  Use the **Board (Kanban) view** for sprint planning sessions. Create columns that match your sprint stages, assign tasks to the sprint, and move cards across columns during your standup to keep everyone aligned in real time.
</Tip>

## Tasks and Subtasks

Tasks are the individual units of work within a project.

**Adding a task:**
1. Open a project and click **+ Add Task** at the bottom of any list or column.
2. Enter a task name and press **Enter** to save it quickly, or click the task to open the detail panel.

**Task details you can set:**
- **Assignee** — Assign to one or more team members.
- **Due Date** — Set a deadline; tasks overdue will be highlighted in red.
- **Priority** — Low, Medium, High, or Urgent.
- **Description** — Add context, instructions, or links.
- **Attachments** — Upload files or link to Google Drive documents.

**Adding subtasks:**
Open any task and click **+ Add Subtask** in the detail panel. Subtasks appear nested beneath the parent task in List view. Completing all subtasks does not automatically mark the parent task as complete — that must be done manually.

**Marking a task complete:**
Click the circle/checkbox to the left of any task name. Completed tasks are visually struck through and can be hidden using the **Hide Completed** toggle.

## Project Status

Every project carries a status that reflects its current state:

- **Active** — Work is ongoing. The project appears in all default dashboard views.
- **On Hold** — Work is paused. The project is visible but marked as inactive.
- **Completed** — All work is done. The project moves to the Completed section of your project list.
- **Archived** — The project is closed and hidden from default views. Data is retained but the project is no longer editable.

To change a project's status, open the project, click the status badge near the project title, and select a new status from the dropdown.

## Milestones

Milestones mark significant points of progress within a project — such as a product launch, a review checkpoint, or the end of a sprint.

**Creating a milestone:**
1. Open the project and navigate to the **Milestones** tab.
2. Click **+ New Milestone**.
3. Enter a name and target date, then click **Save**.

**Tracking milestones:**
Milestones appear as markers on the Calendar view and as a dedicated section in List view. As tasks linked to a milestone are completed, the milestone's progress bar updates automatically. When all associated tasks are done, you can mark the milestone as **Achieved**.

You can link existing tasks to a milestone by opening a task's detail panel and selecting a milestone from the **Milestone** dropdown.

## Archiving and Deleting Projects

**Archiving a project:**
Archiving is recommended when a project is complete and you want to preserve its data without cluttering your active project list.

1. Open the project and click the **⋯** menu next to the project title.
2. Select **Archive Project** and confirm.

Archived projects can be found under **Projects > Archived** and can be unarchived at any time.

**Deleting a project:**
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All tasks, files, and comments within the project will be removed.

1. Open the project and click the **⋯** menu.
2. Select **Delete Project**.
3. Type the project name to confirm, then click **Delete Permanently**.

Only Owners and Admins can delete projects.
