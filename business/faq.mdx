Whether you're just getting started or managing a growing team, this page covers the most frequently asked questions about the Google Apps Business application. If you don't find what you're looking for here, feel free to reach out to our support team.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I sign up for a free trial?">
  Visit [googleapps.com](https://googleapps.com) and click **Get Started Free**. You'll be prompted to create an account using your email address or an existing Google account. No credit card is required to start your free trial. Your trial includes full access to Business-tier features for 14 days.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I invite team members on the free plan?">
  Yes. The free plan allows you to invite up to **3 team members** to your workspace. Each invited member will receive an email prompting them to create an account or sign in. If you need to collaborate with a larger team, consider upgrading to the Pro or Business plan for expanded seat limits.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I upgrade to Pro or Business?">
  To upgrade your plan:

  1. Navigate to **Settings → Billing** in your workspace.
  2. Click **Upgrade Plan**.
  3. Select either **Pro** or **Business** from the available options.
  4. Choose a monthly or annual billing cycle (annual billing saves up to 20%).
  5. Enter your payment details and confirm.

  Your new plan takes effect immediately upon confirmation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
  We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover) via our secure payment processor. Annual Business plans also support **ACH bank transfers** and **invoice-based billing**. To request invoice billing, contact our sales team at [sales@googleapps.com](mailto:sales@googleapps.com).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription at any time?">
  Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription**. When you cancel, your workspace remains active until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account is downgraded to the free plan and your data is retained for 30 days before being permanently removed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How is my data protected?">
  Google Apps takes data security seriously. All data is encrypted **in transit** using TLS 1.2+ and **at rest** using AES-256 encryption. We comply with SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, and CCPA standards. Access controls, audit logging, and role-based permissions help ensure only authorized users can view or modify your data. For more details, see our [Account Settings](/business/account-settings).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I export my data?">
  Yes. You can export your workspace data at any time by going to **Settings → Data Management → Export**. Exports are available in JSON and CSV formats. Large exports are packaged as a `.zip` file and delivered to your account email. If you need a full data archive for compliance purposes, contact [support@googleapps.com](mailto:support@googleapps.com).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I connect to external tools like Slack or GitHub?">
  Integrations can be managed from **Settings → Integrations**. From there, click **Connect** next to the tool you'd like to link (e.g., Slack, GitHub, Jira). You'll be redirected to the third-party authorization flow. Once approved, the integration appears as **Active** and events will begin syncing automatically. See the [Integrations Guide](/business/integrations-overview) for a full list of supported tools.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use the API on the free plan?">
  The API is available on all plans, including the free tier. However, free-plan API usage is subject to rate limits of **100 requests per minute** and **10,000 requests per day**. Pro and Business plans have significantly higher limits. You can generate an API key from **Settings → API Keys**. Review the [API Reference](/business/api-access) for full documentation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many workspaces can I create?">
  - **Free plan:** 1 workspace
  - **Pro plan:** Up to 5 workspaces
  - **Business plan:** Unlimited workspaces

  Each workspace is independent with its own members, projects, settings, and billing. You can switch between workspaces using the workspace switcher in the top-left corner of the dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I exceed my plan limits?">
  If you approach or exceed a plan limit (e.g., seats, storage, or API requests), you'll receive an in-app notification and an email alert. Depending on the limit:

  - **Seat limits:** New invitations will be blocked until you upgrade or remove existing members.
  - **Storage limits:** File uploads will be paused until storage is freed or the plan is upgraded.
  - **API rate limits:** Requests will return a `429 Too Many Requests` error until the rate window resets.

  You can monitor your usage at any time in **Settings → Usage**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?">
  Yes! Google Apps is available on both **iOS** (App Store) and **Android** (Google Play). The mobile app lets you manage projects, view notifications, collaborate with teammates, and access key workspace features on the go. Push notifications keep you up to date in real time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete a workspace?">
  Only an **Owner** can delete a workspace. To do so, go to **Settings → General → Danger Zone** and click **Delete Workspace**. You will be asked to type the workspace name to confirm. This action is **permanent and irreversible** — all projects, members, and data associated with the workspace will be deleted. We recommend exporting your data before proceeding.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Admin and Member roles?">
  | Permission | Admin | Member |
  |---|---|---|
  | Manage billing & plan | ✅ | ❌ |
  | Invite & remove members | ✅ | ❌ |
  | Create & delete projects | ✅ | ✅ |
  | Manage integrations | ✅ | ❌ |
  | Access all workspace projects | ✅ | Depends on project visibility |
  | Manage API keys | ✅ | ❌ |

  **Members** can work within projects they have access to, while **Admins** have full control over workspace-level settings. The workspace **Owner** has all Admin permissions plus the ability to transfer ownership or delete the workspace.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
