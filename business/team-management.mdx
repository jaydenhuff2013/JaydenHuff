Google Apps makes it straightforward to build and manage your team from one central location. You can invite new members, assign them the right level of access, adjust roles as responsibilities change, and remove members who are no longer part of your organization — all from the **Settings > Team** panel.

## Inviting Team Members

<Note>
  Invited members will receive an email invitation with a link to join your workspace. The link expires after 72 hours. If a member doesn't receive the invitation, ask them to check their spam folder or resend the invite from the **Pending Invitations** list.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Team Settings">
    From the left sidebar, click **Settings**, then select **Team** from the Settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    Click the **Invite Member** button in the top-right corner of the Team page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Member's Email">
    In the invitation dialog, type the email address of the person you'd like to invite. You can enter multiple addresses separated by commas to send bulk invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Role">
    Select the appropriate role from the dropdown — **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer**. See the [Team Roles](#team-roles) section below for a full breakdown of each role's capabilities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the Invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. The invitation will appear under **Pending Invitations** until the recipient accepts. You can cancel a pending invitation at any time by clicking **Revoke** next to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Team Roles

Google Apps uses four roles to control what each team member can see and do within a workspace:

| Role       | Capabilities |
|------------|-------------|
| **Owner**  | Full access to all workspace settings, billing, team management, projects, and analytics. Can delete the workspace. Only one Owner is permitted per workspace. |
| **Admin**  | Can invite and remove members, manage roles (except Owner), create and delete projects, and access analytics. Cannot change billing settings. |
| **Member** | Can create and manage tasks within assigned projects, comment, upload files, and view team activity. Cannot manage team membership or workspace settings. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to projects and tasks they have been explicitly added to. Cannot create, edit, or comment on items. |

## Changing Member Roles

To promote or demote a team member's role:

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Find the member in the list and click the **⋯** menu on the right.
3. Select **Change Role**.
4. Choose the new role from the dropdown and click **Confirm**.

The member's permissions update immediately. They will receive an in-app notification informing them of the role change.

## Removing Team Members

To remove a member from your workspace:

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Locate the member you want to remove and click the **⋯** menu.
3. Select **Remove from Workspace**.
4. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.

**What happens to their data:**

- Tasks they created remain in place and are reassigned to the workspace Owner.
- Tasks assigned to them are unassigned and flagged for reassignment.
- Comments and file uploads they made are retained with their name shown as **Former Member**.
- They immediately lose access to the workspace and all its projects.

<Warning>
  You cannot remove the only Owner of a workspace. If you need to transfer ownership, first promote another member to Owner — which will demote the current Owner to Admin — before removing the original Owner's account. Workspaces must always have at least one Owner.
</Warning>

## Team Activity

The **Team Activity** tab within **Settings > Team** gives Owners and Admins a detailed log of actions performed by each team member. You can use this to:

- See which members are most active across projects
- Review recent task completions, status changes, and comments
- Audit role changes and invitations
- Filter activity by a specific member or date range

Click any activity entry to navigate directly to the related task or project. For a broader view of team performance across all projects, visit the [Analytics](/business/analytics) section.
