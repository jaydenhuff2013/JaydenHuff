Before your team can start collaborating, you need to create and configure your Google Apps Business account. This guide covers every part of the setup process — from the initial sign-up form through to security hardening and profile completion — so you can be confident your workspace is ready for day-to-day use.

## Creating Your Account

Navigate to [app.googleapps.com](https://app.googleapps.com) and click **Get Started Free**. You will be presented with a sign-up form containing the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | Your first and last name, used across the platform and in team notifications. |
| **Work Email** | The email address that will serve as your login and receive workspace communications. |
| **Password** | Must be at least 8 characters and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. |
| **Company Name** | The name of your organization, used to pre-fill your workspace name. |

Once you have filled in all fields, click **Create Account** to proceed.

<Warning>
  Keep your login credentials secure. Never share your password with colleagues — use the role-based permissions system to grant access instead. If you believe your account has been compromised, reset your password immediately from **Settings → Security**.
</Warning>

## Email Verification

Google Apps Business requires you to verify your email address before you can access your workspace. This protects your account and ensures communications reach the right person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Your Inbox">
    Open the inbox for the email address you registered with. Look for an email from **noreply@googleapps.com** with the subject line **"Verify your Google Apps Business email"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Verification Link">
    Open the email and click the **Verify Email Address** button. This link is valid for **24 hours**. Clicking it confirms your address and activates your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend if Needed">
    If the email hasn't arrived within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. You can also return to the sign-up confirmation screen and click **Resend Verification Email** to trigger a new message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Workspace Configuration

Once your email is verified, you will be guided through configuring your workspace. These settings establish the identity and default behavior of your workspace for all members.

- **Workspace Name** — Enter the name that will appear in the app header, invitation emails, and exported reports. You can change this later from **Settings → General**.
- **Logo Upload** — Upload a square image (PNG or JPG, minimum 256 × 256 px) to brand your workspace. This logo appears in the sidebar and on notification emails.
- **Timezone** — Select the timezone that best represents where your team is based. This affects how due dates, scheduling, and activity timestamps are displayed by default.
- **Currency** — Choose your preferred currency for any budgeting or billing features within projects. This setting can be overridden on a per-project basis.

Click **Save Workspace Settings** when you are done.

## Inviting Team Members

With your workspace configured, you can start bringing in your team. From the left sidebar, go to **Settings → Members**, then click **Invite Members**.

Enter one or more email addresses and select a role for each invitee. The available roles are:

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full control over the workspace, including billing and deletion. Only one Owner per workspace. |
| **Admin** | Can manage members, configure settings, and access all projects. |
| **Member** | Can create and edit projects and tasks they are assigned to or have been added to. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to projects they have been explicitly added to. |

Click **Send Invitations**. Each person receives an email with a unique join link valid for 7 days. You can resend or revoke pending invitations from the **Members** page at any time.

## Security Settings

Securing your account protects both your workspace and your team's data. Google Apps Business offers two key security configurations:

**Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**

<Note>
  Enabling two-factor authentication is strongly recommended for all workspace members, and required for users with the Admin or Owner role in workspaces on the Business Pro plan.
</Note>

To enable 2FA on your account:

1. Go to **Settings → Security**.
2. Under **Two-Factor Authentication**, click **Enable**.
3. Scan the QR code using an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator or Authy.
4. Enter the six-digit code from your authenticator app to confirm setup.
5. Save your **backup codes** in a secure location — these allow you to recover access if you lose your device.

**Password Requirements**

Google Apps Business enforces the following password policy for all accounts:

- Minimum 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (e.g. `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`)

Passwords expire every 90 days on workspaces with the Security Policy feature enabled. You will receive an email reminder 7 days before expiry.

## Completing Your Profile

A complete profile helps your teammates identify and collaborate with you. To update your profile, click your avatar in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar and select **Edit Profile**.

- **Avatar** — Upload a profile photo (PNG or JPG, minimum 128 × 128 px). Your initials are shown as a placeholder until a photo is uploaded.
- **Display Name** — Set the name that appears on task assignments, comments, and activity notifications. This can differ from the full name on your account.
- **Notification Preferences** — Choose how and when you receive alerts:
  - **Email** — Receive a digest of activity, mentions, and due date reminders.
  - **In-App** — See a notification badge in the sidebar whenever there is new activity.
  - **Frequency** — Choose between real-time, hourly digest, or daily digest for email notifications.

Click **Save Profile** to apply your changes. Your profile is visible to all members of your workspace.
