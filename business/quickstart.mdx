Getting up and running with Google Apps Business is fast and straightforward. This guide walks you through every step from creating your account to exploring your dashboard, so your team can start collaborating right away. Most users complete the full setup in under ten minutes.

<Note>
  Google Apps Business includes a **14-day free trial** with full access to all features — no credit card required. You can invite your entire team and explore everything before choosing a plan.
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Head to [app.googleapps.com](https://app.googleapps.com) and click **Get Started Free**. Fill in your name, work email address, and a secure password, then click **Create Account**.

    Once your account is created, check your inbox for a verification email from Google Apps. Click the **Verify Email Address** link inside to activate your account. If you don't see the email within a couple of minutes, check your spam folder or use the **Resend Verification** option on the confirmation screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Workspace">
    After verifying your email you'll be prompted to create your first workspace. Enter a **workspace name** — this is typically your company or team name and will appear across the app and in any communications sent to your team.

    Next, select the **plan** that best fits your needs. You can start on the free trial and upgrade at any time from your billing settings. Once you confirm your selection, your workspace is created and you'll land on the setup checklist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    On the **Invite Team Members** screen, enter the email addresses of the people you want to bring into your workspace. You can add multiple addresses at once by separating them with commas or pressing **Enter** after each one.

    Assign a **role** to each invitee:
    - **Admin** — full access to workspace settings and all projects.
    - **Member** — can create and edit projects and tasks.
    - **Viewer** — read-only access to projects they are added to.

    Click **Send Invites**. Each person will receive an email with a link to join your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your First Project">
    From the left sidebar, click **Projects**, then select **New Project** in the top-right corner. Give your project a clear **name** and an optional **description** that explains the goal or scope of the work.

    You can also set a **due date** and assign an **owner** at this stage. Click **Create Project** to save it — you'll be taken directly to the project board where you can start adding tasks and milestones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Dashboard">
    Return to the main **Dashboard** by clicking the home icon in the sidebar. Here you'll find:

    - **Activity feed** — a live stream of recent actions taken by you and your team.
    - **My Tasks** — a consolidated view of every task assigned to you across all projects.
    - **Project status cards** — at-a-glance health indicators for each active project.
    - **Upcoming deadlines** — a chronological list of tasks and milestones due soon.

    Take a few minutes to familiarize yourself with the layout. Everything you need to stay on top of your work is accessible from this single view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Set up your **integrations** early — connecting tools like Slack, GitHub, or Google Drive during onboarding means your team gets notifications and file links flowing into the right places from day one. Visit **Settings → Integrations** to browse the available connections.
</Tip>
