Your Google Apps subscription is managed entirely from the Billing section of your account settings. From there you can view your current plan, update your payment method, download past invoices, switch between monthly and annual billing, and manage cancellations. All billing is handled securely — Google Apps does not store raw card numbers on its servers.

## Viewing Your Current Plan

To see which plan you're currently on, your next renewal date, and your current usage:

1. Click your workspace name or avatar in the bottom-left corner.
2. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
3. The **Plan Overview** section shows your active plan, the next billing date, and a usage summary (projects, team members, API calls).

If you are on a team or enterprise plan, only workspace **owners** and **admins** with billing access can view and manage billing information.

## Payment Methods

Google Apps accepts major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) and bank account payments via ACH (US only).

**To add or update a payment method:**

1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Under **Payment Methods**, click **Add Payment Method** or **Update** next to an existing method.
3. Enter your card or bank account details in the secure payment form.
4. Click **Save**. The new method becomes your default for future charges.

You can store multiple payment methods and designate one as the default. Removing your only payment method is not permitted while you have an active paid plan.

## Invoices

A PDF invoice is automatically generated after every successful payment and is available for download at any time.

**To download invoices:**

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Scroll to the **Billing History** section.
3. Click the **Download** icon next to any invoice to save it as a PDF.

Invoices include your workspace name, billing period, itemized charges, payment method last four digits, and a unique invoice number for your records.

## Billing Cycle

You can choose between **monthly** and **annual** billing:

- **Monthly billing** charges your payment method on the same date each month (e.g., if you subscribed on the 15th, you're billed on the 15th of every month).
- **Annual billing** charges the full year upfront at a discounted rate — equivalent to getting **2 months free** compared to monthly.

To switch your billing cycle, go to **Settings > Billing** and click **Change Billing Cycle**. Switching from monthly to annual takes effect at your next renewal date. Switching from annual to monthly takes effect when your current annual period expires.

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails (expired card, insufficient funds, bank decline), Google Apps will:

1. **Notify you immediately** by email with instructions to update your payment method.
2. **Retry the charge** automatically over the next 7 days (on days 1, 3, and 7).
3. **Grant a 7-day grace period** during which your account remains fully active.

If the payment is not resolved within the grace period, your workspace will be downgraded to the Free plan. No data is deleted — your projects and tasks are preserved — but access to paid features will be suspended until a successful payment is made.

To resolve a failed payment, go to **Settings > Billing**, update your payment method, and click **Retry Payment**.

## Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription at any time. There are no cancellation fees.

**To cancel your subscription:**

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**.
2. Scroll to the **Danger Zone** section and click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Select a reason for cancelling (optional) and confirm.

When you cancel:
- Your plan remains active until the **end of your current billing period**. You will not receive a prorated refund for unused time.
- At the end of the period, your workspace is downgraded to the Free plan.
- Data is retained according to the retention policy described below.

<Warning>
  After your subscription is cancelled and your workspace is downgraded, data that exceeds Free plan limits (e.g., projects beyond the 3-project cap) will become **read-only** and will be permanently deleted after **30 days**. Export your data before cancelling if you need to retain it. Go to **Settings > Export** to download a full workspace backup.
</Warning>
