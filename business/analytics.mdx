The Analytics section of Google Apps gives you a comprehensive view of how your team and projects are performing. From high-level business metrics to per-project burndown charts and individual team member performance, the reporting tools are designed to help you make informed decisions and keep work on track.

<Note>
  Analytics data is refreshed every 15 minutes. Metrics shown on the dashboard and in reports may not reflect changes made in the last 15 minutes. For real-time task status, view the project directly.
</Note>

## Overview Dashboard

The Analytics Overview Dashboard presents a summary of the most important metrics across your entire workspace. Use this as your starting point for understanding the overall health of your business operations.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Active Projects** | Total number of projects currently in Active status. |
| **Tasks Completed (This Week)** | Count of tasks marked complete in the current calendar week. |
| **Tasks Overdue** | Number of tasks past their due date that are not yet complete. |
| **Team Velocity** | Average number of tasks completed per team member per week over the last 4 weeks. |
| **Milestone Achievement Rate** | Percentage of milestones reached on or before their target date. |
| **Open Tasks** | Total tasks across all active projects that have not yet been completed. |

Each metric card is clickable and drills down into a more detailed view filtered to that specific metric.

## Project Reports

For a granular look at how individual projects are progressing, navigate to **Analytics > Projects** and select a project from the dropdown.

Each project report includes:

- **Completion Rate** — Percentage of total tasks marked complete, shown as a progress bar and numeric value.
- **Time Tracking** — If time logging is enabled, this shows estimated hours versus actual hours logged per task and in total.
- **Burndown Chart** — A visual chart plotting remaining tasks over time against the expected completion trajectory. Useful for identifying whether a project is on track, ahead, or behind schedule.
- **Task Breakdown by Status** — A donut chart showing the split between To Do, In Progress, In Review, and Done tasks.
- **Milestone Progress** — A timeline of milestones with their achieved vs. target dates.

## Team Performance

The **Team Performance** report (under **Analytics > Team**) gives Owners and Admins visibility into how individual team members are contributing across all projects.

Metrics available per team member:

- **Tasks Completed** — Total tasks marked complete within the selected date range.
- **Tasks Assigned** — Total tasks currently assigned to them.
- **Average Response Time** — Average time between task assignment and first action (e.g., status change or comment).
- **Overdue Tasks** — Count of tasks assigned to them that are past their due date.
- **Projects Active In** — Number of projects they are currently a member of.

Click any team member's row to see a detailed breakdown of their activity, including a task-by-task log.

## Exporting Reports

You can export any analytics report as a **CSV** or **PDF** file for sharing with stakeholders or archiving.

**To export a report:**

1. Navigate to **Analytics** and open the report you want to export (Overview, Project, or Team).
2. Apply any filters you want reflected in the export (see [Date Ranges and Filters](#date-ranges-and-filters)).
3. Click the **Export** button in the top-right corner of the report.
4. Select your preferred format — **CSV** or **PDF**.
5. The file will download to your browser's default download location immediately.

CSV exports include raw data in tabular format, suitable for further analysis in spreadsheet tools. PDF exports include charts and formatted tables, suitable for presentations and reports.

## Date Ranges and Filters

All analytics views support flexible filtering so you can focus on the data that's most relevant to you.

**Date Range:**
Use the date picker in the top-right of any Analytics page to set a custom date range, or choose from presets:
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- This quarter
- Last quarter
- Custom range

**Project Filter:**
Use the **Project** dropdown to limit analytics to one or more specific projects. Useful when you want to compare two projects side by side or focus a report on a client-specific project.

**Team Member Filter:**
Use the **Member** dropdown to filter all metrics and charts to activity from a specific team member. This is particularly useful when reviewing performance for one-on-ones or generating individual contribution reports.

All active filters are shown as tags below the filter bar. Click the **×** on any tag to remove that filter, or click **Clear All** to reset to the full workspace view.

## API Analytics

If your team is using the Google Apps API, API usage metrics are available under **Analytics > API**. This section shows request volumes, error rates, and endpoint-level breakdowns to help you monitor integrations and troubleshoot issues.

For full details on setting up and using the API, see the [API Access](/business/api-access) documentation.
