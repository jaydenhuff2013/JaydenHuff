Google Apps offers four plans designed to grow with your team, from individual contributors getting started for free to large organizations that need enterprise-grade security, support, and customization. Every plan includes the core project and task management features — higher tiers unlock greater limits, advanced integrations, and dedicated support.

<Note>
  All paid plans come with a **14-day free trial** of the Pro plan — no credit card required. You can explore every Pro feature before committing to a subscription.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Switch to annual billing and **save 20%** compared to monthly pricing. Annual billing is available for Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans and can be selected at checkout or changed later in **Settings > Billing**.
</Tip>

### Free

The Free plan is ideal for individuals or very small teams who want to manage projects without any cost. It includes one workspace, up to 3 projects, 5 team members, and community forum support. API access is included at 100 requests/minute. Webhooks and advanced integrations are not available on this tier.

### Pro — $19/month

Pro is built for growing teams that need fewer limits and more connectivity. You get unlimited projects, up to 20 team members, 1,000 API calls/minute, full webhook support, all integrations, and email support. The 14-day free trial lets you explore Pro features without a credit card.

### Business — $49/month

Business removes nearly all limits and adds enterprise-ready features like SSO/SAML, custom integrations, unlimited team members, and priority email support. It's the right choice for teams that need deep customization and faster support response times.

### Enterprise — Custom Pricing

Enterprise is for organizations with complex requirements. It includes everything in Business plus custom limits, a dedicated support manager, on-premise deployment options, negotiated SLAs (up to 99.99% uptime), and security reviews. [Contact our sales team](mailto:sales@googleapps.com) to discuss pricing and requirements.

## Upgrading Your Plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Billing Settings">
    Click your workspace name or avatar in the bottom-left corner, then navigate to **Settings > Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Upgrade Plan">
    Under **Plan Overview**, click **Upgrade Plan** to open the plan selection page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Plan">
    Choose the plan you'd like to upgrade to. You'll see a summary of what's included and the price difference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Billing Cycle">
    Select **Monthly** or **Annual** billing. Annual billing saves 20% and is charged as a single upfront payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and Pay">
    Review the order summary, confirm your payment method, and click **Confirm Upgrade**. Your new plan is active immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downgrading Your Plan

You can downgrade to a lower plan at any time from **Settings > Billing > Change Plan**.

When you downgrade:

- The change takes effect at the **end of your current billing period** — you keep all current features until then.
- After the period ends, your workspace is adjusted to the new plan's limits. Projects, tasks, and members that exceed the new limits become **read-only**.
- If you exceed the new plan's team member cap, additional members will lose access until the count is within the limit.
- Webhooks and integrations unavailable on the lower plan will be automatically disabled, though their configurations are saved for 30 days in case you re-upgrade.

Review your current usage before downgrading to understand which resources may be affected.
