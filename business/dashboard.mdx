The Google Apps business dashboard is your central hub for monitoring projects, tracking team activity, and taking action on the things that matter most. Whether you're checking in on a project's progress or responding to a new notification, everything you need is available at a glance the moment you log in.

## Dashboard Overview

When you first land on the dashboard, you'll see a set of widgets organized into a default layout designed to surface the most relevant information for your team. These widgets include:

- **Projects Summary** — A high-level count of active, on-hold, and completed projects.
- **Tasks Due Soon** — Tasks assigned to you (or your team) with upcoming due dates.
- **Team Members** — A snapshot of active team members and their current availability status.
- **Recent Activity** — A live feed of recent actions taken across your workspace.
- **Performance Metrics** — Key stats like tasks completed this week and overall project health.

Each widget links directly to the relevant section of the product so you can dive deeper with a single click.

## Activity Feed

The Activity Feed widget displays a real-time log of everything happening across your workspace. This includes:

- Team members joining or leaving projects
- Task assignments, completions, and status changes
- Comments and @mentions on tasks or projects
- File uploads and document updates
- Milestone reached notifications

You can filter the feed by **All Activity**, **My Activity**, or activity tied to a specific **Project** using the dropdown at the top of the feed. Click any feed item to jump directly to the relevant task, project, or conversation.

## Quick Actions

The Quick Actions bar sits at the top of your dashboard and provides one-click shortcuts to the most common tasks in Google Apps. Available quick actions include:

- **+ New Project** — Opens the project creation dialog immediately.
- **+ New Task** — Create a standalone task or assign it to an existing project.
- **Invite Member** — Send an invitation to a new team member.
- **View Reports** — Navigate directly to the Analytics section.
- **Search** — Open the global search to find anything across your workspace.

Quick Actions adapt based on your role. Owners and Admins see additional shortcuts such as **Manage Billing** and **Workspace Settings**.

## Notifications

The notification bell icon in the top-right corner of the dashboard shows unread alerts. Clicking it opens a notification panel with items such as:

- Task assignments and due date reminders
- @mentions in comments
- Project status changes
- Team invitations accepted or declined

**Managing notification preferences:**

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **Notifications** tab.
3. Toggle individual notification categories on or off.
4. Choose your delivery method: **In-app**, **Email**, or both.
5. Set a **Digest** schedule if you prefer a daily or weekly summary rather than real-time alerts.

Changes to notification preferences are saved automatically.

## Customizing Your Dashboard

You can tailor the dashboard layout to match the way you work. Google Apps supports drag-and-drop widget reordering and several personalization options.

**Reordering widgets:**

1. Hover over any widget to reveal the drag handle (⠿) in the top-left corner.
2. Click and hold the handle, then drag the widget to your preferred position.
3. Release to drop the widget into place — the layout saves automatically.

**Pinning projects:**

1. Navigate to the **Projects** section from the left sidebar.
2. Find the project you want to pin and click the **⋯** menu.
3. Select **Pin to Dashboard**.
4. The project card will now appear in the **Pinned Projects** widget on your dashboard.

**Adding and removing widgets:**

Click the **Customize** button (grid icon) in the top-right of the dashboard to open the widget panel. Check or uncheck widgets to show or hide them. You can restore the default layout at any time by clicking **Reset to Default**.
