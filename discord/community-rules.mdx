The Costum Stuff Discord is a space for shoppers, creators, and fans to talk about custom items, share ideas, and help each other out. These rules keep the community welcoming for everyone.

## Community rules

1. **Be respectful** Treat every member with kindness. No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. **No spam** Do not flood channels with repeated messages, excessive emoji, or off-topic content. Keep conversations readable and useful.
3. **No self-promotion without permission** Do not post links to your own store, stream, or social media unless a moderator has given explicit approval.
4. **Keep NSFW content out** The Costum Stuff community is open to all ages. Do not post or link to adult, graphic, or otherwise inappropriate material.
5. **Follow Discord Terms of Service** All members must abide by the official [Discord ToS](https://discord.com/terms). This includes age requirements and platform-wide rules.

<Warning>
  Breaking these rules can result in a warning, temporary mute, or permanent ban depending on severity and repeat behavior.
</Warning>

## Need help or want to report something?

If you see rule-breaking behavior or have a concern, tag a moderator in the **#support** channel or send a direct message to any online mod. Please include screenshots or message links when possible so the team can act quickly.

<Note>
  Moderators reserve the right to remove content or restrict access at their discretion to preserve a positive environment.
</Note>
