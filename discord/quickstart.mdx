This quickstart walks you through everything you need to go from zero to a fully operational Google Apps bot in your Discord server. By the end you will have created your Google Apps account, connected a Discord application, invited the bot, completed the setup wizard, and verified the integration with a test command. The entire process takes around five minutes.

<Note>
  You must have the **Manage Server** permission on the Discord server you want to add the bot to. If you do not see this permission in your Discord role settings, ask a server administrator to grant it or to complete the invite on your behalf.
</Note>

## Setup Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Google Apps Account">
    If you do not already have a Google Apps account, head to [app.googleapps.com](https://app.googleapps.com) and sign up for free.

    1. Click **Get Started** on the homepage.
    2. Enter your email address and choose a password.
    3. Verify your email using the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
    4. Once verified, you will land on the Google Apps dashboard — this is your central hub for all bot configuration.

    <Tip>
      If your organisation already has a Google Apps workspace, ask your administrator to invite you as a member instead of creating a new account.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a Discord Application">
    The Google Apps bot uses a Discord application to communicate with your server. You need to create one in the Discord Developer Portal.

    1. Go to the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and log in with your Discord account.
    2. Click **New Application** in the top-right corner.
    3. Give your application a name (for example, *Google Apps Bot*) and click **Create**.
    4. In the left sidebar, select **Bot** and then click **Add Bot**, confirming with **Yes, do it!**
    5. Copy the **Token** — you will need this in Step 4. Treat it like a password and never share it publicly.
    6. Under **Privileged Gateway Intents**, enable **Server Members Intent** and **Message Content Intent**, then click **Save Changes**.

    <Warning>
      Your bot token grants full control over the bot account. If it is ever exposed, immediately click **Reset Token** in the Developer Portal to invalidate the old one.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Invite the Bot to Your Server">
    With your Discord application created, generate an invite link and add the bot to your server.

    Navigate to **OAuth2 → URL Generator** in the Developer Portal. Under **Scopes**, select:
    - `bot`
    - `applications.commands`

    Under **Bot Permissions**, select the permissions the bot needs to function:

    | Permission | Why It's Needed |
    |---|---|
    | Manage Roles | Assign and remove roles for auto-role rules |
    | Kick Members | Execute kick actions from moderation commands |
    | Ban Members | Execute ban actions from moderation commands |
    | Manage Messages | Delete messages flagged by auto-mod |
    | View Audit Log | Read server audit events for logging |
    | Send Messages | Post welcome messages and command responses |

    The generated invite URL will look like this:

    ```
    https://discord.com/api/oauth2/authorize?client_id=YOUR_CLIENT_ID&permissions=1099645117504&scope=bot%20applications.commands
    ```

    Replace `YOUR_CLIENT_ID` with the **Application ID** found on the **General Information** page of your Discord application. Open the URL in your browser, select your server from the dropdown, and click **Authorise**.

    <Note>
      If you prefer a simpler setup, you can select the **Administrator** permission instead of individual permissions. This grants full access to the server — convenient for testing, but not recommended for production environments.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the Setup Wizard">
    Back in the Google Apps dashboard, the bot setup wizard will guide you through the final configuration.

    1. In the dashboard sidebar, click **Discord** → **Connect Server**.
    2. Paste the **Bot Token** you copied in Step 2.
    3. The dashboard will automatically detect your server and display it in the **Connected Servers** list.
    4. Click **Configure** next to your server to open the setup wizard.
    5. Follow the wizard prompts to set your:
       - **Log channel** — where audit events are posted
       - **Welcome channel** — where new member greetings appear
       - **Mod role** — the role that can run moderation commands
    6. Click **Save & Finish** to apply the configuration.

    <Tip>
      You can revisit and change any of these settings at any time from **Discord → Server Settings** in the dashboard.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test the Bot with /ping">
    Confirm everything is connected by running a test command directly in Discord.

    1. Open Discord and navigate to any text channel in your server where the bot has **Send Messages** permission.
    2. Type `/ping` and press **Enter**.
    3. The bot should respond with a message similar to:

    ```
    🏓 Pong! Latency: 42ms | API: 38ms
    ```

    If the bot responds, your setup is complete. If the `/ping` command does not appear in the slash command menu, wait up to one hour for Discord to propagate the command registration, then try again.

    <Note>
      Slash commands can take up to **one hour** to appear globally after first registration. If you're in a hurry, register them to a specific guild for instant availability — the setup wizard includes this option.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>
