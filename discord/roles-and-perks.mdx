Costum Stuff rewards active community members with special Discord roles that unlock extra channels, early access, and recognition. Roles are assigned automatically when you link your Discord account, based on your activity and purchase history.

## Role overview

| Role | How you get it | Key perks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Member** | Join the server | Access to public chat, announcements, and general channels |
| **Customer** | Make any purchase on Costum Stuff | Entry to customer-only channels and drop reminders |
| **VIP** | Become a repeat buyer | Early access to new drops, VIP-only channels, and priority support |
| **Beta Tester** | Opt in via the #roles channel | Test upcoming products, give direct feedback, and help shape future releases |

<Info>
  The Customer, VIP, and Beta Tester roles require your Discord account to be linked to your Costum Stuff profile. Visit the [Link Account](/discord/link-account) page to set that up.
</Info>

## What each perk means

- **Early drops:** See new products and restocks before they go live on the main store.
- **Exclusive channels:** Chat in spaces reserved for verified customers and VIPs.
- **Feedback role:** Beta Testers get sneak peeks and a direct line to share thoughts on prototypes.

<Tip>
  You can opt in or out of the Beta Tester role anytime by visiting the #roles channel and toggling the reaction role.
</Tip>
