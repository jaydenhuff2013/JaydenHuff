Linking your Discord account to Costum Stuff lets the server automatically assign you roles that reflect your shopping history. Once connected, you gain access to exclusive channels and early announcements without any manual verification.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Profile">
    Log in to the Costum Stuff website and click your name in the top-right corner, then choose **Profile** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    In the left sidebar of your Profile page, select **Integrations**. You will see a list of available services you can connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Discord">
    Find the Discord row and click **Connect**. A Discord authorization popup will appear asking you to approve Costum Stuff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the app">
    In the popup, review the permissions, then click **Authorize**. You will be redirected back to Costum Stuff once the connection is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your Discord roles update within a few minutes based on your purchase history. If you do not see a role change right away, try refreshing Discord or logging out and back in.

<Tip>
  You only need to link once. Future purchases will automatically update your roles.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Make sure you are logged into the correct Discord account in your browser before you click Connect. If you link the wrong account, visit Profile > Integrations > Discord and choose **Disconnect**, then repeat the steps.
</Warning>
