Google Apps' auto-role system takes the manual work out of role management. Whether you want every new member to receive a guest role the moment they join, let members self-assign roles by reacting to a message, or reward long-time members automatically, you can configure it all without writing a single line of code.

<Warning>
  The Google Apps bot's own role must be positioned **higher** in the server's role hierarchy than any role it is configured to assign. If the bot's role sits below a target role, Discord will reject the assignment and the action will fail silently.
</Warning>

## Join Roles

Join roles are assigned automatically the instant a new member enters your server. This is ideal for granting access to introductory channels, applying a "new member" tag, or kicking off a verification flow.

You can assign **multiple join roles** simultaneously. All configured join roles appear in a list under **Auto-Roles → Join Roles** in the dashboard, and each can be enabled or disabled individually without being deleted.

## Reaction Roles

Reaction roles let members self-assign or remove roles by clicking an emoji reaction on a designated message. This is a popular way to let members opt into interest-based channels (e.g., reacting 🎮 to get a `#gaming` role).

To set up a reaction-role message:

1. Navigate to **Auto-Roles → Reaction Roles** in the dashboard.
2. Click **Create Reaction Role Message**.
3. Choose the channel where the message will be posted.
4. Write your message text explaining the available roles.
5. Add one or more emoji–role pairs (e.g., 🎮 → `Gamer`, 🎵 → `Music Lover`).
6. Select a behavior mode:
   - **Toggle** — Clicking the reaction adds the role; clicking again removes it.
   - **Add Only** — Clicking adds the role; removing the reaction does nothing.
   - **Unique** — Only one role from the group can be held at a time.
7. Click **Post Message** to publish it to the selected channel.

The bot will send the message and automatically attach all configured emoji reactions.

## Timed Roles

Timed roles are assigned after a member has been in the server for a specified number of days. This is useful for creating a "verified member" tier or unlocking additional channels once trust is established.

Configure timed roles under **Auto-Roles → Timed Roles**. For each rule you define:

- The **role** to assign
- The **number of days** the member must have been in the server
- An optional **notification message** sent to the member when the role is assigned

The bot checks membership duration on a daily cycle, so role assignments may occur up to 24 hours after the threshold is reached.

## Role Conditions

Role conditions let you assign roles based on specific criteria evaluated when a member joins or at a scheduled interval. Available conditions include:

- **Account Age** — Only assign a role if the member's Discord account is older than a specified number of days. Useful for reducing bot and raid accounts from accessing certain channels.
- **Profile Completeness** — Assign a role only if the member has an avatar set on their account.
- **Invite Source** — Assign different roles depending on which invite link the member used to join.

Multiple conditions can be combined with AND/OR logic from the dashboard rule builder.

## Managing Auto-Roles in the Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Auto-Roles Panel">
    Log in to the Google Apps dashboard, select your server, and navigate to **Auto-Roles** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Role Type">
    Select the tab for the type of auto-role you want to configure: **Join Roles**, **Reaction Roles**, **Timed Roles**, or **Role Conditions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a New Rule">
    Click **Add Rule** (or **Create Message** for reaction roles). Fill in the required fields — role selection, trigger, and any optional conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Enable">
    Click **Save**. New rules are enabled by default. You can toggle any rule on or off at any time using the switch next to its entry in the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the Configuration">
    Use a secondary account or ask a trusted member to test the flow. Confirm the role appears in their profile immediately (join roles) or after the appropriate trigger (reaction, timer, condition).
  </Step>
</Steps>
