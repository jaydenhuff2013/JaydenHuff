Google Apps can connect your Discord server to the platforms your community already follows. Once an integration is configured, the bot automatically posts alerts and content updates to the channel of your choice — so your members never miss a live stream, a new video, a tweet, or a blog post.

<Note>
  All integrations use Discord webhooks to post messages. The bot requires the **Manage Webhooks** permission in any channel where you want integration notifications to appear. Without this permission, the bot cannot create the webhook and the integration setup will fail.
</Note>

## Setting Up Twitch Alerts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Twitch account">
    In the [Google Apps dashboard](https://app.googleapps.example.com), navigate to your server's **Integrations** tab and click **Connect Twitch**. You'll be redirected to Twitch's OAuth page — authorize Google Apps to read your channel information. If you're setting up alerts for someone else's channel, you only need their Twitch username, not account access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Twitch channel">
    After authorizing, click **Add Channel** under the Twitch Alerts section. Enter the Twitch streamer's username and click **Search** to confirm the channel exists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Discord channel">
    Select the Discord channel where live alerts should be posted from the **Post alerts to** dropdown. The bot will create a webhook in that channel automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the alert message">
    Customize the alert embed using the message template editor. Available variables include `{streamer}`, `{title}`, `{game}`, `{viewers}`, and `{url}`. Enable or disable the **@everyone** / **@here** mention toggle based on your community preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Click **Save Integration**, then click **Send Test Alert** to preview the embed in your chosen channel. Adjust the template if needed and save again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## YouTube Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a YouTube channel">
    Go to **Integrations → YouTube** in the dashboard and click **Add YouTube Channel**. Paste the channel's YouTube URL or channel ID into the search field and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the notification channel">
    Choose the Discord channel where new video notifications should be posted. The bot checks for new uploads approximately every 10 minutes using YouTube's public feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the notification (optional)">
    Optionally edit the notification message template. Available variables: `{channel}`, `{title}`, `{url}`, `{thumbnail}`. You can also toggle separate alerts for **new videos** vs. **live streams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the integration">
    Click **Save**. The bot will begin monitoring the YouTube channel for new content and post to Discord automatically on the next detected upload.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Twitter/X Feed

Configure a Twitter/X feed integration to mirror public tweets into a Discord channel.

1. Navigate to **Integrations → Twitter/X** in the dashboard.
2. Enter the Twitter/X username to monitor (without the `@`).
3. Choose which tweet types to include: **Original tweets**, **Replies**, **Retweets**, **Media only**.
4. Select the destination Discord channel and customize the embed template using `{username}`, `{tweet}`, `{url}`, and `{media}`.
5. Click **Save Integration**.

<Note>
  Twitter/X feed monitoring depends on X's API availability. During periods of API instability or rate-limit enforcement by X, there may be delays in new tweets being posted. Google Apps will resume normal posting when API access is restored.
</Note>

## RSS Feeds

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add an RSS URL">
    In the dashboard, go to **Integrations → RSS Feeds** and click **Add Feed**. Paste the full RSS or Atom feed URL into the input field and click **Validate**. The bot will verify the URL is reachable and returns a valid feed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the posting channel">
    Select the Discord channel where new feed entries should be posted. Each entry will be displayed as an embed showing the item title, description excerpt, and a link to the full post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the check interval">
    Choose how often the bot should check the feed for new items. Options: **5 minutes**, **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**. Higher-traffic feeds may benefit from a shorter interval; low-traffic feeds (such as weekly newsletters) can use a longer one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Click **Save Feed**. The bot will fetch the feed on the next interval and post any new items it hasn't seen before. Existing entries at the time of setup are not backfilled.
  </Step>
</Steps>
