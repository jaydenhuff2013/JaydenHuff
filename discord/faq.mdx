Whether you're just getting started or running into something unexpected, this page covers the most frequently asked questions about the Google Apps Discord bot. Browse through the topics below to find quick, clear answers.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I invite the bot to my server?">
  To invite the Google Apps Discord bot to your server, you must have the **Manage Server** permission on the target server. Navigate to the **Integrations** section of your Google Apps dashboard, click **Add to Discord**, and follow the OAuth2 authorization flow. Select your server from the dropdown, confirm the requested permissions, and click **Authorize**. The bot will appear in your server's member list once the process is complete.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What permissions does the bot need?">
  The bot requires a core set of permissions to function correctly:

  - **View Channels** — to read channel activity and respond in text channels
  - **Send Messages** — to post responses, welcome messages, and notifications
  - **Manage Roles** — to assign or remove roles from members
  - **Kick Members / Ban Members** — to execute moderation commands
  - **View Audit Log** — to populate the server log channel
  - **Embed Links & Attach Files** — to send rich embeds and file attachments

  You can review and adjust these permissions at any time under **Server Settings → Integrations → Google Apps Bot** in Discord.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The bot says it doesn't have permission — what do I do?">
  This error typically means the bot is missing one or more Discord permissions in the specific channel or server role. To resolve it:

  1. Go to **Server Settings → Integrations → Google Apps Bot** and verify all required permissions are enabled.
  2. Check the individual channel's permission overrides — a channel-level deny can override a server-level allow. Navigate to the channel settings and ensure the bot's role is not explicitly denied any needed permissions.
  3. Make sure the bot's role sits **above** any roles it needs to manage in the role hierarchy.

  After adjusting permissions, retry the command.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I set up a welcome channel?">
  1. In your Google Apps dashboard, open your server's **Configuration** page.
  2. Under **Welcome Messages**, click **Set Channel** and choose the channel where you want welcome messages to appear.
  3. Customize the welcome message text using the available placeholders (e.g., `{user}` for the new member's mention, `{server}` for the server name).
  4. Click **Save**. The bot will now greet every new member in the selected channel.

  Make sure the bot has **Send Messages** and **View Channel** permissions in that channel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use the bot on multiple servers?">
  Yes! There is no hard limit on the number of servers you can add the Google Apps bot to, as long as your current plan supports it. Each server maintains its own independent configuration — welcome channels, role assignments, moderation settings, and log channels are all stored separately per server. Refer to your plan's documentation for any server-count limits that may apply to your subscription tier.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I reset the bot's configuration?">
  To reset all bot settings for a server back to their defaults, go to your Google Apps dashboard, select the target server, scroll to the bottom of the **Configuration** page, and click **Reset to Defaults**. You will be asked to confirm the action. Note that this will erase all customizations — welcome messages, log channels, role mappings, and command settings — and cannot be undone.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if the bot goes offline?">
  If the bot goes offline, it will not respond to commands or send automated messages (such as welcome messages or log entries) until it reconnects. Any events that occur while the bot is offline — such as new member joins — will not be retroactively processed once it comes back online. You can check the current status of the bot on the [Google Apps Status Page](https://status.googleapps.com). If an outage is not listed and the bot remains offline, try re-inviting it to your server or contact support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I assign different commands to different roles?">
  The Google Apps bot supports role-based command access controls. To configure this:

  1. Open your server's **Configuration** page in the Google Apps dashboard.
  2. Navigate to the **Command Permissions** tab.
  3. For each command or command category, use the role selector to specify which roles are allowed or denied access.
  4. Click **Save Changes**.

  Members without an allowed role will receive a permission error if they attempt to run a restricted command.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I customize the bot's username and avatar?">
  The bot's global username and avatar are managed by Google Apps and cannot be changed by individual server administrators. However, you can use Discord's built-in **Server Profiles** feature (available on servers that have been boosted to Level 1 or above) to set a server-specific nickname for the bot. Right-click the bot in the member list, select **Edit Server Profile**, and enter your preferred nickname.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I report a bug or request a feature?">
  We welcome bug reports and feature requests from the community:

  - **Bug reports**: Email [support@googleapps.com](mailto:support@googleapps.com) with a description of the issue, the steps to reproduce it, and any relevant screenshots or error messages.
  - **Feature requests**: Use the **Feedback** button inside the Google Apps dashboard, or post in the **#feature-requests** channel in the official Google Apps Discord support server.

  Our team reviews all submissions and will follow up if further information is needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I remove the bot from my server?">
  To remove the bot, go to your Discord server's **Server Settings → Integrations**, locate the Google Apps bot, and click **Remove**. Alternatively, you can right-click the bot in the member list and select **Kick**. Note that kicking the bot will remove it immediately, but your configuration data will be retained in the Google Apps dashboard for 30 days in case you wish to re-add it later.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is my server's data private?">
  Yes. Google Apps only collects the data necessary to operate the bot's features — such as server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and configuration settings. We do not read or store the content of your members' messages. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is never sold to third parties. For full details, please review our [Privacy Policy](https://googleapps.com/privacy).
</Accordion>
