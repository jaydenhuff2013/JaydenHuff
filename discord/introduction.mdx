The Google Apps Discord bot is your all-in-one community management companion. Whether you're running a small hobby server or a large enterprise community, the bot handles moderation, automates role assignments, greets new members, and keeps a detailed audit trail — all from within Discord. This page introduces what the bot can do and points you toward the resources you need to get started.

## What Is the Discord Bot?

The Google Apps Discord bot is a first-party integration between the Google Apps platform and Discord. Once invited to your server, it connects to your Google Apps workspace and exposes a rich set of slash commands, automation rules, and management tools. You control everything through the Google Apps dashboard or directly in Discord — no separate app required.

The bot operates in real time, reacting to server events as they happen. Whenever a member joins, a message is deleted, or a role is assigned, the bot can take automatic action, log the event, or notify your moderation team — keeping your community safe and organised with minimal manual effort.

## How It Works

When you invite the Google Apps bot to your server, it authenticates with the Google Apps platform using OAuth2 and registers your server inside your workspace. From that moment, the bot listens to Discord Gateway events — member joins, message activity, role updates, and so on — and maps them to automation rules you define in the dashboard.

Each action the bot takes is scoped to the permissions you grant at invite time. You can start with a minimal set of permissions and expand them later as you enable new features. All configuration changes sync instantly between the dashboard and the live bot, so there is no need to restart or re-invite after updates.
