The Costum Stuff Discord is the best place to hang out with other fans, get early news on new products, and share photos of your custom gear. You can find the invite link on the Costum Stuff website footer or inside your order confirmation email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the invite link">
    Open the Costum Stuff website and scroll to the footer, or check your latest order confirmation email. Click or copy the invite link labeled "Join our Discord."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Discord">
    Launch the Discord app on your computer or phone, or open [discord.com](https://discord.com) in your browser. Make sure you are signed in to the Discord account you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the invite">
    Paste the invite into Discord's "Join a Server" field, or click the invite link directly. Review the server preview, then tap **Accept Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your roles">
    If prompted, select any optional interest roles (for example, apparel, accessories, or prints) so you see the channels you care about most.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You will land in the #welcome channel. Say hello, read the pinned rules, and start exploring.

<Note>
  If the invite link does not work, it may have expired. Grab a fresh one from the Costum Stuff website or email [support@costumstuff.example](mailto:support@costumstuff.example) for a new link.
</Note>
