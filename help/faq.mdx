Get fast answers to the most common questions shoppers ask about Costum Stuff. If you need more help, visit [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) or [Contact Support](/help/contact-support).

<Accordion title="How long does standard shipping take?">
  Standard shipping typically takes 5 to 10 business days after dispatch, depending on your location. You will receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order leaves our facility.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the turnaround for custom orders?">
  Custom orders usually take 7 to 14 business days to produce before shipping. Turnaround varies based on design complexity and current queue volume. You will receive an estimated completion date after submitting your request.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is your refund and return policy?">
  Non-custom items can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. Custom items are final sale unless they arrive defective or damaged. In that case, contact support with photos and your order number.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do Discord roles sync with my store account?">
  After you [link your Discord account](/discord/link-account), our system checks your purchase history and assigns roles automatically. Repeat buyers receive the VIP role, and eligible members may receive the Beta Tester role. Role updates can take up to a few hours.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I change my order after placing it?">
  You can request changes within 2 hours of placing an order by contacting support with your order number. After that window, production or shipping may have already started, and modifications may not be possible.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I cancel an order?">
  Orders can be canceled within 2 hours of placement if they have not yet entered production. Go to your [Order History](/account/order-history) and click **Request Cancellation** on the order. Custom orders that have already been started cannot be canceled.
</Accordion>
