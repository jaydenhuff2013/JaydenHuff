Run into a snag while shopping or using the Discord community? Use the guides below to resolve the most common Costum Stuff issues on your own before reaching out to support.

<Accordion title="I can't log in to my account">
  First, double-check that your email and password are correct. If you forgot your password, use the **Forgot Password** link on the login page and follow the reset email instructions. If you still cannot log in, clear your browser cache and try again, or use an incognito window.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My payment was declined">
  Verify that your card details and billing address match what your bank has on file. Check that your card has not expired and that you have sufficient funds. If using PayPal, confirm that your PayPal account is in good standing. You can also try a different payment method from your [Payment Methods](/account/payment-methods) page.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My tracking number is not updating">
  Tracking information can take 24 to 48 hours to appear after dispatch. If it has been longer, confirm the carrier has received the package. Sometimes scans are missed during transit; the next update usually appears at the destination hub. If there is no movement after 5 business days, contact support with your order number.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My Discord role was not applied after linking">
  Make sure you completed the full [account linking process](/discord/link-account). Role assignments can take up to a few hours to sync. Ensure your Discord account is a member of the Costum Stuff server. If the role still does not appear after 24 hours, unlink and re-link your account, then contact support if the issue persists.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I did not receive an order confirmation email">
  Check your spam or promotions folder first. If it is not there, confirm the email address on your [Profile](/account/profile) is correct. You can also view the order in your [Order History](/account/order-history). If the order appears there but the email is still missing, contact support and we will resend it.
</Accordion>

<Note>
  If an issue is not listed here, or these steps do not resolve it, reach out through [Contact Support](/help/contact-support).
</Note>
