If you cannot find an answer in the [FAQ](/help/faq) or [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) guides, the Costum Stuff support team is ready to help. Providing the right details upfront speeds up the response and gets you back to shopping or chatting in Discord faster.

## Support channels

<CardGroup>
  <Card title="Discord #support" icon="message-circle">
    Post in the **#support** channel on the Costum Stuff Discord server. This is best for quick questions, role issues, and community-related problems.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Email" icon="mail">
    Send a message to [**support@costumstuff.example**](mailto:support@costumstuff.example). Use this channel for order issues, returns, billing disputes, and anything that requires privacy.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What to include in your request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the issue clearly">
    Explain what you expected to happen and what actually happened. Include error messages or unusual behavior.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide your order number">
    If your issue relates to a purchase, include the order number from your [Order History](/account/order-history). This lets support look up your transaction immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach screenshots">
    Add screenshots of error messages, missing Discord roles, payment failures, or anything visual that helps illustrate the problem.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List steps you have already tried">
    Mention any troubleshooting steps you attempted so support does not ask you to repeat them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For fastest help with order issues, use email and include your order number in the subject line.
</Tip>

## Result

Your support request is submitted with all the context needed for a quick, accurate reply.
