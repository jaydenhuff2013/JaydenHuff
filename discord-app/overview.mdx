## Core experience

A Discord-style app works best when it is simple to join and easy to navigate. New members can enter a server, browse a few key channels, and immediately know where to ask questions, share updates, or connect with friends.

- **Server-first organization** keeps communities structured without feeling crowded.
- **Channel-based communication** makes it easy to separate topics and keep discussions focused.
- **Real-time messaging** supports quick collaboration and fast event updates.
- **Voice and video rooms** add a human layer to community engagement.
