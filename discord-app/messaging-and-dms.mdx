A community app feels alive when messaging remains fast and intuitive. People should be able to send a quick update, reply to a thread, or move a conversation into a private DM without friction.

## Core messaging features

### Direct messages

Use direct messages for one-to-one conversations, support follow-ups, or private coordination between members.

### Threads

Threads keep long conversations from cluttering the main channel. Use them for bug reports, project discussions, or detailed Q&A.

### Reactions and mentions

Reactions help people acknowledge updates without forcing a long reply. Mentions keep attention on urgent messages or requests.

### File sharing

Members should be able to share screenshots, mockups, and attachments quickly. Keep the upload flow simple and visible.

## Good messaging habits

- Start threads when a topic needs more than a quick reply.
- Use concise subject lines in support or feedback channels.
- Pin answers that help people repeatedly.
- Keep DMs professional and respectful, especially in support workflows.

## Example workflow

A customer sees a product issue, posts in the bug channel, and a support agent replies in a thread. The conversation stays organized while the original post remains readable for everyone else.
