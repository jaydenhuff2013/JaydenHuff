Bots make a community app feel more useful. They can handle reminders, moderation checks, role assignments, and onboarding flows without adding friction for your team.

## Useful bot features

- custom slash commands
- welcome messages for new members
- reminder and announcement automation
- role assignment for onboarding or events
- moderation alerts and reports

## Good command design

Commands should be simple and obvious. Examples:

- `/help` for common actions
- `/rules` to show server guidelines
- `/report` to submit a moderation issue
- `/event` to create reminders or schedule updates

## Keep automation lightweight

Bots are most effective when they solve repetitive tasks. Avoid overloading the app with too many rules or complicated automations.

## Best practices

- Keep commands visible to users.
- Limit high-impact actions to moderators.
- Test bot behavior before enabling it for the full community.
- Use logs to track bot actions and unexpected errors.
