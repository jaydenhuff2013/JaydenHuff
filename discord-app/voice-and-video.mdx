Voice chat is one of the biggest reasons people stay active in a Discord-style app. It adds personality and makes your community feel more human.

## Common voice use cases

- live product demos
- office hours or Q&A sessions
- community events and AMAs
- casual hangout rooms for members

## Recommended setup

Start with a few simple voice channels, such as:

- **Lobby** for people joining before a session begins
- **General Voice** for casual conversation
- **Events** for scheduled community calls

## Voice features to include

- push-to-talk or instant join options
- screen sharing for demos and walkthroughs
- mute controls and noise management
- stage or speaker moderation for live events

## Best practices

- Keep the first voice rooms simple and easy to join.
- Use clear naming so members know the purpose of each room.
- Announce events ahead of time in your announcement channel.
- Give moderators tools to manage speakers and attendance.
