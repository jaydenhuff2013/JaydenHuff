If your app is running smoothly, members can focus on conversations instead of technical friction. These are the most common problems to expect as the community grows.

## Common issues

### Members cannot join a server

Check the invite link, verify expiration settings, and confirm the member has the correct access level. If you require verification, make sure they complete the flow.

### Notifications are missing

Ask users to turn on desktop and mobile notifications. Also confirm that the app is not muted and that the channel is not grouped into a quiet mode setting.

### Voice chat is not working

Verify microphone permissions, check browser or app access, and confirm the user is connected to the right voice channel. Audio issues are often caused by permissions or a bad device selection.

### Messages are being missed

Users may be in a quiet server or a muted channel. Encourage them to review notification settings and pin the most important channels in their sidebar.

## Improve the support experience

- Create a clear support channel for help requests.
- Keep a pinned onboarding guide for new members.
- Use bots or templates to answer frequent questions quickly.
