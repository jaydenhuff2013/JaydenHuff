## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your application">
    Decide whether you want to set up the Discord server bot, the Business application, or both. Each has its own dedicated documentation section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.googleapps.com](https://app.googleapps.com) with your email address. One account gives you access to both applications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the Quickstart">
    Use the Quickstart guide for your chosen application to get up and running in minutes. For Discord, that means inviting your bot. For Business, it means creating your first workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore features">
    Dive deeper into moderation tools, team management, analytics, and integrations once your initial setup is complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>
