Costum Stuff lets you save payment methods so you can check out faster without re-entering details every time. You can manage cards and PayPal from one place in your account settings.

## Add a payment method

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Payment Methods">
    Log in and open **Account Settings**, then select the **Payment Methods** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your method">
    Click **Add Payment Method** and pick **Credit or Debit Card** or **PayPal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter and save">
    Fill in the required fields. For cards, provide the number, expiry, and CVV. For PayPal, you will be redirected to authorize the connection. Click **Save** when finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Update or remove a method

- To **update** a saved card, click the pencil icon next to it, edit the details, and confirm.
- To **remove** any method, click the trash icon and confirm the deletion.

<Warning>
  You cannot delete your default payment method without first setting another method as default.
</Warning>

## Security note

Costum Stuff uses industry-standard encryption and tokenization to store payment details. Full card numbers are never stored on our servers; they are handled by our PCI-compliant payment partner.

## Result

Your saved payment methods are ready for quick, secure checkout on future orders.
