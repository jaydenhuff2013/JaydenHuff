Your order history on Costum Stuff is the central place to review everything you have purchased. From here you can track deliveries, download invoices, re-order items, and begin returns when eligible.

## View past orders

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Order History">
    Log in and click your avatar in the top-right corner, then choose **Order History**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse and filter">
    Scroll through your list of orders, or use the search bar and date filters to find a specific purchase. Each entry shows the order number, date, total, and current status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View order details">
    Click any order to expand it. You will see item names, quantities, shipping address, and tracking information if the order has been dispatched.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download invoices

Inside any order detail view, click **Download Invoice** to save a PDF copy for your records. Invoices include item breakdowns, taxes, and shipping costs.

## Re-order an item

If you want the same product again, open the order and click **Re-order**. This adds the item to your cart with the same options selected previously. You can adjust quantities or variants before checking out.

## Start a return

For non-custom items within the 14-day return window, click **Start Return** on the order detail page. Custom items are final sale unless defective. Follow the prompts to select the item, reason, and preferred resolution.

<Tip>
  If you do not see a **Start Return** button, the item may be outside the return window or ineligible. Contact support for help with defective custom items.
</Tip>

## Result

You can now manage your entire purchase history from one page, from receipts to re-orders.
