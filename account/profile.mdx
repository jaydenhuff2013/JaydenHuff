Your Costum Stuff profile is the hub for your personal details and how the store communicates with you. Keeping it current ensures smooth checkout, accurate shipping, and timely order updates.

## Edit your profile info

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account settings">
    Log in to Costum Stuff and click your avatar in the top-right corner. Select **Account Settings** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update basic info">
    In the **Profile** tab, edit your display name, email address, and phone number. Click **Save Changes** before leaving the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload or change your avatar">
    Click your current avatar image, then choose a new file from your device. For best results, use a square image under 5 MB.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage notification preferences

In the **Notifications** tab, choose how Costum Stuff reaches you:

- **Order updates**: shipping confirmations, dispatch notices, and delivery alerts
- **Promotions**: new drops, sales, and exclusive Discord events
- **Community**: replies to your custom order requests and forum mentions

Toggle each channel on or off, then click **Save Preferences**.

<Info>
  Email notifications are sent to the address on your profile. Make sure it is up to date so you do not miss tracking numbers or role-sync confirmations.
</Info>

## Result

Your profile now reflects your latest details, and you receive only the notifications you want.
